# The spell tables (`spells/data/`)

The spell tables. `SpellMgr` and its `SpellMgr*.cpp` parts load the spell data (chains, areas, pet auras, proc events, the lookups derived from the DBC files) and answer what a spell is. They are the data layer: nothing here reads a unit, a player or a cast, or sends a packet. The rules that read them for a caster (the cast time and duration a caster gets, whether a spell may be cast where a player stands, the area spells a player gains or loses) are in `spells/pipeline/SpellRules.h`. A spell-ID label that only looks something up ends here as a table. Include its headers by their path from `src/game`.
