# The data stores (`data/`)

The stores here hold world data loaded once from the world database at start-up (and again on a `.reload`, for a table that has one) and then only read, through lookups. A store is the container and its lookups: it names no entity, session, packet or database type, so the stores build and are tested in `mangos_tests` with no database. The loader that reads the table and validates its rows stays with `ObjectMgr` until the loaders move; `ObjectMgr` owns each store and its getter forwards to the lookup, except the two script stores, which are reached through their own accessors and filled by `ScriptMgr`'s loaders.

| Store | Holds |
|---|---|
| `MailLevelRewardStore` | the mail a character is sent on reaching a level (`mail_level_reward`): per level, the rewards in the order loaded, each for the races in its mask; `ObjectMgr::LoadMailLevelRewards` clears and fills it (at start-up, and on `.reload mail_level_reward` or `.reload all`) and `ObjectMgr::GetMailLevelReward` forwards to its lookup |
| `FishingBaseSkillStore` | the base fishing skill level of an area (`skill_fishing_base_level`): one value per area id, which may be negative, a second value for an area overwriting the first; `ObjectMgr::LoadFishingBaseSkillLevel` clears and fills it (at start-up, and on `.reload skill_fishing_base_level` or `.reload all`) and `ObjectMgr::GetFishingBaseSkillLevel` forwards to its lookup |
| `ExplorationBaseXpStore` | the base XP of exploring an area of a given level (`exploration_basexp`): one value per area level, a second value for a level overwriting the first; `ObjectMgr::LoadExplorationBaseXP` fills it at start-up only (no `.reload` reads the table) and `ObjectMgr::GetBaseXP` forwards to its lookup |
| `MapGeometry` | not a store: a map's grid and cell geometry (the grid and cell sizes and counts, `GridPair` and `CellPair`, the grid or cell a position falls in) and the validity checks: a coordinate (`MaNGOS::IsValidMapCoord`), a map id the server can load (`MaNGOS::IsValidMapId`: in Map.dbc, and a dungeon only with an instance template) and a position on such a map (`MaNGOS::IsValidMapPosition`); the loaders check their rows with it, and `GridDefines.h` and `MapManager`'s checks build on it |
| `PointOfInterestStore` | the points of interest (`points_of_interest`): the map pins a gossip menu can show, one per id, a second point for an id overwriting the first; `ObjectMgr::LoadPointsOfInterest` clears and fills it (at start-up, and on `.reload points_of_interest`, `.reload all_npc`, `.reload all_gossips` or `.reload all`) and `ObjectMgr::GetPointOfInterest` forwards to its lookup; the point names per locale stay with the locales |
| `QuestStore` | the quest templates (`quest_template`), one per id, which the store owns and deletes, and the exclusive quest groups, each in the order added; `ObjectMgr::LoadQuests` clears it, adds every quest and edits them in place while it links their chains, and `ObjectMgr` answers its lookups |
| `QuestRelationStore` | who starts and ends each quest (`quest_relations`): by creature or game object entry, the quests it starts and the quests it ends, in the order loaded, and the game object entries that activate for quests; `ObjectMgr`'s relation loaders and `LoadGameObjectForQuests` fill it (at start-up and on the quest relation reloads), and `ObjectMgr` answers its lookups |
| `AreaTriggerStore` | the area triggers: the teleports (`areatrigger_teleport`), one destination and its requirements per trigger id, a second teleport for a trigger overwriting the first; the quest each objective trigger serves (the area trigger rows of `quest_relations`); and the tavern triggers (`areatrigger_tavern`); `ObjectMgr::LoadAreaTriggerTeleports`, `ObjectMgr::LoadQuestAreaTriggers` and `ObjectMgr::LoadTavernAreaTriggers` each clear and fill their own part (at start-up, and on `.reload areatrigger_teleport`, `.reload areatrigger_quest_end`, `.reload areatrigger_tavern`, `.reload all_area`, `.reload all_quest` or `.reload all`), and `ObjectMgr::GetAreaTrigger`, `ObjectMgr::GetQuestForAreaTrigger` and `ObjectMgr::IsTavernAreaTrigger` forward to its lookups |
| `GameTeleStore` | the named teleport locations (`game_tele`), one per id, a second location for an id overwriting the first; `ObjectMgr::LoadGameTele` clears and fills it (at start-up, and on `.reload game_tele` or `.reload all`), `ObjectMgr::AddGameTele` and `ObjectMgr::DeleteGameTele` add and erase one for `.tele add` and `.tele del`, and `ObjectMgr::GetGameTele` and `ObjectMgr::GetGameTeleMap` forward to it |
| `GraveyardStore` | the graveyards linked to each zone (`game_graveyard_zone`): per zone id, the links in the order added, each a graveyard and the team it serves, a graveyard linked to a zone at most once; `ObjectMgr::LoadGraveyardZones` clears and fills it (at start-up, and on `.reload game_graveyard_zone`, `.reload all_area` or `.reload all`), `ObjectMgr::AddGraveYardLink` adds a link (and `.linkgrave` one to the table) and `ObjectMgr::SetGraveYardLinkTeam` edits a link's team through the store's edit access as an outdoor PvP zone changes hands, and `ObjectMgr::GetClosestGraveYard` and `ObjectMgr::FindGraveYardData` read it |
| `GossipMenuStore` | the gossip menus (`gossip_menu`) and their options (`gossip_menu_option`): per menu id, the texts the menu may show, each with its script and condition, and the options it offers, each in the order loaded, the options of menu id 0 being the default ones; `ObjectMgr::LoadGossipMenu` and `ObjectMgr::LoadGossipMenuItems` each clear and fill their own part (at start-up, and on `.reload gossip_menu`, `.reload gossip_menu_option`, `.reload all_gossips` or `.reload all`), and `ObjectMgr::GetGossipMenusMapBounds` and `ObjectMgr::GetGossipMenuItemsMapBounds` forward to its lookups |
| `NpcTextStore` | the npc texts (`npc_text`): the texts a gossip menu shows, one per id, each its options with their language, probability and emotes; `ObjectMgr::LoadGossipText` fills each text it reads in place (at start-up, and on `.reload npc_text`), so a reload overwrites the texts it reads and keeps the others, and `ObjectMgr::GetGossipText` forwards to its lookup |
| `CreatureSpawnStore` | the creature spawns (`creature`), one `CreatureData` per spawn guid; `ObjectMgr::LoadCreatures` fills it at start-up only (no `.reload` reads the table), each spawn in place through `FindOrAdd`, as `Creature::SaveToDB` does for a spawn added or moved in game and `MapManager::LoadActiveEntities` for an active spawn; `ObjectMgr::DeleteCreatureData` erases one; `ObjectMgr::GetCreatureDataPair` and `ObjectMgr::GetCreatureData` forward to its lookup, and `ObjectMgr::DoCreatureData` walks every spawn |
| `CreatureClassLevelStatsStore` | the creature base stats (`creature_template_classlevelstats`): for each level, creature class index (`classToIndex`) and expansion, the base health, mana, damage, attack powers and armor; `ObjectMgr::LoadCreatureClassLvlStats` clears it and sets each row's four expansions at start-up only (no `.reload` reads the table), and `ObjectMgr::GetCreatureClassLvlStats` reads a creature's expansion, answering nothing for a creature with no expansion (-1) or a slot with no health |
| `CreatureModelRaceStore` | the racial models (`creature_model_race`): per native model id, the models that replace it for the races in each entry's mask, in the order loaded; `ObjectMgr::LoadCreatureModelRace` clears and fills it at start-up only (no `.reload` reads the table), and `ObjectMgr::GetModelForRace` reads it |
| `SpellClickStore` | the spell click spells (`npc_spellclick_spells`): per creature entry, the spells a player clicking the creature casts, each with its quest or condition, in the order loaded; `ObjectMgr::LoadNPCSpellClickSpells` clears and fills it (at start-up, and on `.reload npc_spellclick_spells`, `.reload all_npc` or `.reload all`), and `ObjectMgr::GetSpellClickInfoMapBounds` forwards to its lookup |
| `GameObjectSpawnStore` | the game object spawns (`gameobject`), one `GameObjectData` per spawn guid; `ObjectMgr::LoadGameObjects` fills it at start-up only (no `.reload` reads the table), each spawn in place through `FindOrAdd`, as `GameObject::SaveToDB` does for a spawn added or moved in game; `ObjectMgr::DeleteGOData` erases one; `ObjectMgr::GetGODataPair` and `ObjectMgr::GetGOData` forward to its lookup, and `ObjectMgr::DoGOData` walks every spawn |
| `CellObjectGuidStore` | the spawn guids filed by where they load: per map, spawn mode and grid cell, the creature and game object spawns and the player corpses in that cell (`CellObjectGuids`), and per map the active creature spawns, which load with the map; `ObjectMgr`'s grid edits (`AddCreatureToGrid`, `AddGameobjectToGrid`, their removals and the corpse cell edits) file and unfile them, `ObjectMgr::LoadCreatures` adds the active creatures and `ObjectMgr::DeleteCreatureData` removes a deleted one, `ObjectMgr::GetCellObjectGuids` answers a cell for the grid loader, adding an empty one, `ObjectMgr::GetCellObjectGuidsReadOnly` answers it without adding one, for `.grid info`, and `ObjectMgr::GetActiveCreatureGuids` answers the active creatures for the map's start-up |
| `ItemConvertStore` | the item conversions: the item a vendor sells to another race or team in place of an item (`item_convert`) and the item an item becomes when its duration runs out (`item_expire_convert`), one target per item entry each; `ObjectMgr::LoadItemConverts` and `ObjectMgr::LoadItemExpireConverts` each clear and fill their own part (the race conversions at start-up and on `.reload item_convert`, `.reload all_item` or `.reload all`; the expire conversions at start-up only), and `ObjectMgr::GetItemConvert` (which also checks the target serves the race) and `ObjectMgr::GetItemExpireConvert` read it |
| `ItemRequiredTargetStore` | the item required targets (`item_required_target`): per item entry, the creatures the item's spell may be used on, each alive or dead (`ItemRequiredTarget`, in `ItemPrototype.h`), in the order loaded; `ObjectMgr::LoadItemRequiredTarget` clears and fills it (at start-up, and on `.reload item_required_target`, `.reload all_item` or `.reload all`), and `ObjectMgr::GetItemRequiredTargetMapBounds` forwards to its lookup |
| `PlayerCreateInfoStore` | the player create info and the XP of each level: per race and class, the `PlayerInfo` (`playercreateinfo` and its item, spell and action lists, and the stats per level from `player_levelstats`, whose array the store owns and deletes), and the XP each level needs for the next (`player_xp_for_level`), 0 past the last level; `ObjectMgr::LoadPlayerInfo` fills both at start-up only (no `.reload` reads the tables), editing each info in place, and `ObjectMgr::GetPlayerInfo`, `ObjectMgr::GetPlayerLevelInfo` and `ObjectMgr::GetXPForLevel` read it |
| `PetLevelInfoStore` | the pet stats per level (`pet_levelstats`): per creature entry, one `PetLevelInfo` per level, level 1 first, in an array the store owns and deletes; `ObjectMgr::LoadPetLevelInfo` fills it at start-up only (no `.reload` reads the table), each entry's array in place, then fills the level gaps, and `ObjectMgr::GetPetLevelInfo` reads it |
| `ReservedNameStore` | the reserved character names (`reserved_name`), held lower case; `ObjectMgr::LoadReservedPlayersNames` clears and fills it (at start-up, and on `.reload reserved_name` or `.reload all`), and `ObjectMgr::IsReservedName` asks it with the lower-case name |
| `PetNameStore` | the pet name parts (`pet_name_generation`): per creature entry, the first and the second halves of a random pet name, each in the order loaded; `ObjectMgr::LoadPetNames` fills it at start-up only (no `.reload` reads the table), and `ObjectMgr::GeneratePetName` picks one of each, or the family name when an entry has none |
| `ReputationStore` | the reputation tables: the reward rates per faction (`reputation_reward_rate`), the reputation a kill gives per creature entry (`creature_onkill_reputation`) and the spillover templates per faction (`reputation_spillover_template`), the records in `ReputationRecords.h`; `ObjectMgr::LoadReputationRewardRate` and `ObjectMgr::LoadReputationSpilloverTemplate` clear and fill their part (at start-up, and on `.reload reputation_reward_rate` and `.reload reputation_spillover_template`), `ObjectMgr::LoadReputationOnKill` fills its part at start-up only, and `ObjectMgr::GetRepRewardRate`, `ObjectMgr::GetReputationOnKillEntry` and `ObjectMgr::GetRepSpilloverTemplate` forward to its lookups |

Includes of the files directly here are path-qualified (`#include "data/MailLevelRewardStore.h"`): this directory is on no target's include path. Its two subdirectories are on the data target's, so their headers are included by name: `dbc/` holds the client data (the DBC and DB2 stores and their structures, `SharedDefines.h`), `world/` the world tables (`ObjectMgr` and its loaders, the item prototypes and their lookup by name, the loot store, the quest definitions, the game events, the pools, the waypoints, the creature links and the disables). `CheckLayout` classifies every file here as data (`src/tests/tools/layout_gate.py`'s `RULES`), and `CheckHeaderReach` keeps each store's header from reaching `ObjectMgr.h`, the entities, the session, the world, the database and the packets.
