# The character (`Player`)

`Player.h` and `Player.cpp` hold the class itself: construction, the update loop, the fields and the glue between the parts below. `PlayerRegistry` finds the online characters by guid or name. Everything else the character does lives in one directory per concern:

| Directory | Concern |
|---|---|
| `persistence/` | loading the character from the database, saving it, and offline lookups through the character cache |
| `quests/` | the quest log, quest status and the rules for taking and completing a quest |
| `talents/` | talents, specs and glyphs |
| `spells/` | the spell book, spell modifiers, learning, action buttons, cooldowns and runes |
| `inventory/` | items: storage, equipping and its stat effects, enchants and gems, equipment sets, durability, gear score, and currencies |
| `interaction/` | what the character does with NPCs and the post: gossip, vendors, loot and mail |
| `combat/` | melee, combo points, death and resurrection, duels, derived stats, regeneration, and the XP and honor rewarded at kills and group events |
| `pvp/` | the PvP flag and contested state, battlegrounds and honor |
| `social/` | groups, channels, chat, reputation, and the friend and ignore lists |
| `world/` | movement, zones and areas, area triggers, instances, visibility, rested XP, breath and fatigue timers and environmental damage, and taxi routes |
| `pets/` | the character's pets, their action bars and the pet cache read at login |

Includes name the file only (`#include "Player.h"`, `#include "TalentMgr.h"`): every directory here is on the `game` target's include path, so a header's name must stay unique across that path.

## Managers and the owner side

`src/tests/CheckManagerIsolation.cmake` puts every file in this tree, subdirectories included, on exactly one of two lists; a file on neither fails `ctest`.

- **`MANAGER_FILES`** are managers: a piece of the character's state and the rules over it, built and tested in `mangos_tests` without a character. A manager never names the character class: no include of `Player.h`, no pointer, reference, member or alias of the class, and not the word itself, comments included. It takes everything it needs (guid, name, game time, template lookups) as parameters. `src/tests/CheckHeaderReach.cmake` also keeps each manager's includes from reaching `Player.h`, `Unit.h`, the session or the object manager.
- **`OWNER_FILES`** are the character's own side: `Player.h`, `Player.cpp`, every `Player*.cpp`, `PlayerRegistry`, `PlayerTaxi`, `PlayerPetCache` and this README, plus the older managers that still name the class until each one is converted.

The owner side of a concern lives in the same directory as its manager: `quests/PlayerQuest.cpp` drives `quests/QuestStatusMgr` (and computes a quest reward's arithmetic -- XP, money, the chosen item -- through the pure functions of `quests/QuestRewardRules`), `talents/PlayerTalent.cpp` drives `talents/TalentMgr`, `talents/PlayerGlyph.cpp` drives `talents/GlyphMgr` (the glyphs of each spec), the `inventory/PlayerItem*.cpp` files store into, and ask, `inventory/InventoryMgr` (the slot array, the lookups and counts over it, the storage checks -- can this item go here -- and the bank check), `spells/PlayerRune.cpp` drives `spells/RuneMgr` (a death knight's runes), `spells/PlayerSpellCooldown.cpp` drives `spells/SpellCooldownMgr` (the spell cooldowns), `pets/PlayerPet.cpp` drives `pets/PetMgr` (the stable slots and the pet a temporary unsummon put away; the live pet itself stays with the owner), `social/SocialMgr` -- a global, since a status change goes to every character whose list names the changed one -- drives `social/SocialList` (`PlayerSocial`, a character's friend and ignore list, and the online-visibility verdict), `inventory/PlayerCurrency.cpp` drives `inventory/CurrencyMgr` (the currency counts, their caps, the weekly reset and the currency packets), `social/PlayerReputation.cpp` drives `social/ReputationMgr` (the faction list, its standings and flags, the spillover and the rank counters; the changes that send packets or call back into the character go through the owner's wrappers, `Player::SetReputation`, `ModifyReputation`, `SetFactionVisible` and `SetFactionAtWar`), and `pvp/PlayerHonor.cpp` drives `pvp/HonorMgr` (the daily kill rollover and a kill's honor: the rules over the character's, the victim's and the realm's facts that the owner reads, and the credit packet).

A manager that used to read or write the character takes what it read as parameters, computed by the owner just before the call, and makes each write through a callback it calls where the old code wrote: a packet it builds goes to a sink (`Player::SessionSink()`, the character's session), a field or an aura change to a callback the owner supplies. `spells/RuneMgr` is the first of these.

## Manager shape

The rules every manager converted in decoupling D4k follows; `spells/RuneMgr` and `spells/SpellCooldownMgr` are the examples.

1. **State plus rules, nothing else.** The manager is default-constructed, never names the character class, and reaches neither `Player.h`, `Unit.h`, `WorldSession.h`, `Spell.h`, `SpellAuras.h` nor `ObjectMgr.h` (`CheckHeaderReach`). A pointer it only stores and hands back (a rune's convert `Aura`) stays opaque, never dereferenced; one it would only pass through to the owner (the cast's `Spell`) stays inside the owner's callback.
2. **Reads are inputs.** What the old body read from the character is a parameter, computed by the owner's wrapper with the old expression just before the one call. The clock is one of them: the owner reads `time(NULL)` and passes `now`, so a test passes a fixed time. Three or more facts or sinks go in one small struct (`RuneMgr::ConvertAuraFacts`, `SpellCooldownMgr::CastInputs`). A fact read again in the middle of a call, after the owner's own callbacks have run, is a read callback called at each old point instead of a value (`CurrencyMgr::CurrencyInputs::canNotify`, the in-world-and-not-loading check; `ReputationMgr::FlagNotify::playerLoading`). **The one exception, copies of facts no game path changes:** a fact that no game path changes while the character is in memory (the manager's class comment names any way outside the game paths, such as an administrator's raw field write, and what then differs) may be copied into the manager once, by the owner's creation and its load, when the getters that read it have outside callers that would otherwise all need the fact as a parameter. `social/ReputationMgr` holds the race and class masks and the name this way (`SetOwnerFacts`); its class comment names every writer of the three and the one that is left out (an administrator's raw field write).
3. **Writes are call-scoped callbacks.** Each write to the character is a `std::function` the manager calls at the old statement, the same number of times with the same arguments. A callback is a parameter only, never stored in a member.
4. **Packets are built in the manager** and handed to the one sink type every manager shares (`ManagerPacketSink.h`), which the owner's `SessionSink()` returns: the session is read at each send, the path `SendDirectMessage` takes too.
5. **The owner side lives in the concern's `Player*.cpp`.** Decoupling D4i drops, domain by domain, every forwarder that only passes its own arguments through to a manager method of the same signature (D4i-1: the eight D4k managers and the talents; D4i-2: the quest status queries, the status map, `RemoveTimedQuest` and the weekly/monthly resets, while the verdict wrappers stay because they send the refusal; D4i-3: the inventory lookups, the counts, `IsValidPos`, `GetItemDisplayIdInSlot` and the static position checks, whose qualified calls now name the class (`InventoryMgr::IsBagPos(pos)`) while their calls on a character go through the accessor; the storage and bank checks stay because they pass what the manager cannot read: `_CanTakeMoreSimilarItems` the item template and limit category lookups, `_CanStoreItem` and `CanStoreItems` those and the binding check, `CanBankItem` those, the binding check, the bank bag count and the use check): callers reach the manager through the character's accessor (`GetRuneMgr()`, `GetSpellCooldownMgr()`, `GetPetMgr()`, `GetCurrencyMgr()`, `GetTalentMgr()`, `GetReputationMgr()`, `GetQuestStatusMgr()`, `GetInventoryMgr()`), the character's own members call the member directly, and `src/tests/tools/forwarder_rewrite.py` does the rewrite. One exception stays on purpose: `GetPetCache()` (const and not) remains the accessor of the pet rows that every cache reader uses, although `GetPetMgr().GetPetCache()` reaches the same object. A forwarder whose inputs are plain expressions (`time(NULL)`, the guid, `SessionSink()`) stays inline in `Player.h`; one that needs a lambda or a header `Player.h` does not reach moves out of line there, with file-local sink factories. Orchestration over the character's own state (a cast, the potion bookkeeping) stays on the owner, verbatim. The load is per row (`LoadRow`, the owner keeps the loop) and the save takes the guid; the manager's files are on `CheckSyncDb`'s list, and its packets and tables are a `CheckStateOwnership` row.
6. **A manager's members are defined in the manager's own files.** A member may be defined in an owner file only when an outside caller that may not change pins its signature and the member needs the character (today one: `PlayerSocial::SendSocialList()`, defined in `social/SocialMgr.cpp` because `MiscHandlerSocial.cpp` and the character's login packets call it without arguments). Its declaration says so and warns that it does nothing in `mangos_tests`, and it is listed in `CheckManagerIsolation.cmake`'s `OWNER_DEFINED` allowlist: the gate fails on any other definition of a manager class's member outside `MANAGER_FILES`, on a second one of a listed member, and on an entry that no longer matches a definition.
